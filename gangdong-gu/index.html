01

The recommendation in numbers

Gangdong-gu has about 500,000 residents. Heating still relies mainly on gas boilers, and buildings cause 71% of the district's emissions. We modelled three pathways and recommend District Energy Consideration: heat pumps, a 5th-generation heating and cooling network, battery storage and coordinated EV charging.

03

The Gangdong Energy Hub

A public-private Energy-as-a-Service platform finances, owns and runs the equipment. Residents subscribe to heat, cooling, electricity and EV charging at regulated tariffs, with no upfront investment.

Tap a tier to see who owns what.

Public tier · the backbone

Long-lived assets with broad public value, co-owned by Gangdong-gu, the Seoul Metropolitan Government, KDHC and KEPCO.

  • 5GDHC heating and cooling network
  • Central thermal storage
  • Central heat pumps
  • Grid modernisation

Private tier · customer-facing assets

Run by one operating company. Equity follows each operator's share of the investment:

  • Building and HVAC operator: about 75%
  • PV operator: about 15%
  • EV charging operator: about 7%
  • Battery and flexibility operator: about 3%
